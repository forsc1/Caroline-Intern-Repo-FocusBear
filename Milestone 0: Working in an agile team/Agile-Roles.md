# Reflection

## What is your role, and how does it contribute to the Agile process?
My role is as a frontend developer intern. My main responsibility is to contribute to the user-facing parts of the Focus Bear application while learning and following the team's existing development practices. This means I'm involved with implementing frontend changes, fixing bugs, testing my work, submitting pull requests, responding to code review feedback etc.
My role contributes to the Agile process by completing small, clearly defined pieces of work that can move through the team's workflow and be reviewed before being integrated into the product.


## How do your responsibilities overlap or interact with other roles in the team?
My responsibilities also overlap with several other roles. For example, I may work with UX team members to understand how a feature should behave with other developers during code reviews, and with QA when testing whether a feature works as expected.


# Task

## Write a short description of how your role fits into the Agile team.
As a frontend developer, I help turn requirements and high level designs into working features that users can interact with. I contribute by taking tasks from the team's workflow, implementing and testing changes, and responding to feedback from other team members.

## Identify one other role you frequently collaborate with and describe how you can improve teamwork with them.
One role I expect to collaborate with frequently is other developers, particularly through pull requests and code reviews. I can improve teamwork by making my pull requests clear and focused by explaining the changes I have made and testing my work before requesting a review.
