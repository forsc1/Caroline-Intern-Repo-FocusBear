# Reflection 

## Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?
When I worked at Beachcomber Restaurant in the St Kilda Sea Baths Complex, it was my first time working with a team in a very chaotic customer facing environment. I was able to learn how to communicate effectively with my co-workers to ensure we were executing tasks efficiently whilst managing high expectations for customer satisfaction through our professionalism. What made it effective was our ability to communicate fast and professionally by remaining calm under the pressure of a fast-paced environment, and looking out for the well-being of team members to make sure everyone was feeling supported.


## What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?
Remaining highly professional and considerate of the tasks and pressure of my team is top priority. This can be done by understanding the projects and expectations each member of my team is currently working on when I communicate with them. Furthermore, I will be completely transparent about any issues I have with a project or task and let my supervisor know straight away if I need help.

## How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?
I will always remain considerate of my team, and bring a positive attitude to any project I am working on. I will never put down my co-workers or neglect communication with them for long periods of time, and I will always stay kind and passionate about my work.

## How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?
Making sure I prepare and review my notes I've made before the meeting by writing down any questions I have on my assigned tasks. Furthermore, reviewing what I've completed of the assigned tasks and reviewing any relevant team updates or project progress.

## What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?
Maintaining consistent communication throughout the duration of my internship through Discord, stand-ups and co-working days. Making sure I'm completing allocated tasks before deadlines and reaching out to my supervisor if I'm struggling to complete certain tasks.

## How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?
I would first contact them through Discord and raise the issue during a stand-up meeting or in person on a co-working day. If I still did not receive a response and the issue was preventing me from completing my work, I would follow up with my supervisor and escalate it to Jeremy if necessary.

## What are some things you won't do?
I will not cut communication from my supervisor or team for long periods of time during my internship. I will not neglect weekly stand-up meetings and wait until the deadline to ask for help for allocated tasks and projects. I will also not come to meetings unprepared and ask the same questions multiple times.
