## How I Work Best
- I do my best work when I have clear expectations and enough context about a task before I begin. I like breaking larger or unfamiliar problems into smaller steps and working through them methodically. I work well when I can spend focused time on a task without too many interruptions.
- I struggle when expectations are unclear or when I am unsure what the priority of a task is. Asking questions and clarifying the task early helps me avoid this.

## Communication Style
- I prefer messages to be clear and direct, while still giving enough detail for me to understand the context.
- For most everyday communication, I prefer asynchronous messages through Discord because I can refer back to them while I am working. I also find stand-ups and quick discussions useful when something is easier to explain verbally.
- The best way to reach me is through Discord or during a stand-up.
- If you need something urgently, tag me on Discord or send me a direct message and make it clear that it is urgent.

## Collaboration & Teamwork
- I work best in a team when everyone communicates consistently, asks for help when they need it and keeps each other updated on progress or blockers. I appreciate teammates who are supportive and willing to share their knowledge.
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is when communication stops completely and nobody knows what is happening with a task.
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am listening and processing what is being discussed. I often like to think about an idea before responding to it. I try to prepare for meetings by reviewing my progress, relevant team updates and any questions I want to ask beforehand.

## Focus & Productivity
- I stay focused by breaking my work into clear tasks and working through them one at a time.I like having a structured to do list so I know what I need to prioritise. When working with an unfamiliar repo, I prefer to first read the documentation, understand the project structure and look at relevant existing code before making changes.
- Things that distract me include frequent interruptions and trying to work on too many unrelated things at once.
- The best way to support my productivity is to give me clear priorities and enough uninterrupted time to work through a task.

## Feedback & Growth
- I like feedback to be clear, direct, preferably private, and constructive, especially when examples are included. Written feedback is most helpful because I can refer back to it while making changes.
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is to explain what needs to change and why. I would rather know about an issue early so I can fix it and learn from it.
- I appreciate it when teammates share their knowledge with me, answer questions and explain how they approach a problem. I am particularly interested in learning through code reviews and feedback from more experienced developers.

## Fun Facts & Quirks
- One thing people often misunderstand about me is that being quiet does not necessarily mean I am disengaged. I may just be listening or thinking through what is being discussed.
- A fun fact about me is that outside of software development, I am interested in music and songwriting.
- My work superpower is breaking unfamiliar problems into smaller pieces and gradually figuring out how everything fits together.
