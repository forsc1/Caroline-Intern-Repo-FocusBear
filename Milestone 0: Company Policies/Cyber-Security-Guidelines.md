# Reflection

## What security measures do you currently follow, and where can you improve?
I currently use two-factor authentication where possible and avoid accessing important accounts through unsecured public networks. I also make sure not to share passwords or login credentials with other people. An area I can improve is being more consistent with reviewing my passwords and making sure I am using unique passwords across different platforms. I can also be more aware of locking my devices whenever I step away from them.

## How can you make secure behaviour a habit rather than an afterthought?
I can make secure behaviour a habit by including basic security checks in my normal work routine. For example, I can lock my computer whenever I leave my workspace, check links before opening them, and review files before uploading or committing them. By consistently doing these things every time I work, they will become part of my normal routine rather than something I only think about when a security issue occurs.

## What steps will you take to ensure your passwords and accounts are secure?
I will use a unique and strong password for each of my work accounts and enable two-factor authentication wherever it is available. I will also review how I currently store my passwords and consider using a password manager if I am not already using a secure method. I will never share passwords or login credentials with another person, including other team members.

## What would you do if you suspected a security breach or suspicious activity on your account?
If I noticed suspicious activity or an unexpected login, or anything else that could indicate a security breach, I would report it immediately rather than ignoring it. I would contact my supervisor or the appropriate person at Focusbear and explain what I noticed. If necessary, I would change the affected password and secure the account while following any further instructions given by the team.
